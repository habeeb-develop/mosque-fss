Who We Are

Serving the Ummah

FSSSMC Central Mosque is a place dedicated to worship, Islamic learning, community development and service.

We strive to provide an environment where Muslims can worship Allah, acquire beneficial knowledge, strengthen relationships and contribute positively to society.

  • Daily congregational prayers
  • Jummah services
  • Islamic education
  • Community programmes
  • Spiritual guidance
