About the Mosque

A place of worship, knowledge, peace, unity and community service.

Our Purpose

Serving the Ummah through worship, knowledge and service.

FSSSMC Central Mosque exists as a place for congregational worship, Islamic learning, community engagement and service to the Ummah.

The mosque's work is rooted in the Qur'an and Sunnah and supported by a leadership structure responsible for administrative and spiritual decision-making and approval.

  • Congregational prayer and Jummah service
  • Islamic education and learning
  • Community welfare and service
  • Da'awa and community engagement
  • Masjid maintenance and development
